Street View Hint
Version 1.5

Summary (132 chars max)
Shows the country, compass direction, and city for the current WorldGuessr round.

Description
See where you are in WorldGuessr — without spoiling the fun.

Street View Hint adds a quiet overlay on worldguessr.com with three clues for the current round:

• Country
• Compass area (northwest, central, southeast, and the rest)
• City

Toggle only what you want. Open the extension popup to turn each piece on or off:

• Hints — the corner overlay
• Drop pin on the map — places your guess near the real spot
• Zoom to the area — centers the map for you

Closest and Farthest control how far the pin may sit from the true location. Leave them at 10 km and 20 km for a ring around the spot, or set both to 0 km for the exact point. After the pin drops, you can still move it — the extension never presses Guess.

How it works
The extension reads the round from the Street View embed, looks up place names with OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and shows a compass sector that matches how the guess map is laid out. Large cities are measured from the city center; smaller places from the country center. Coordinates stay off the screen.

A line of … means the lookup is still running. “na” means that part is not available yet (for example, no city in the data).

Privacy & attribution
No account and no API key. Lookups are cached on your computer and paced to one request per second. Place names and borders are © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
