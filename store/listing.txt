Street View Hint
Version 1.1.0

Summary
Shows the country, compass direction, and city for the current WorldGuessr round.

Description
Street View Hint reads the current WorldGuessr round and shows three things: the country, a compass direction, and the city.

You can turn each part on or off:
- Hints in the corner of the page
- A guess pin on the map
- A zoom toward that area

Closest and Farthest set how far the pin may sit from the real location. At 10 km and 20 km, the pin lands somewhere in that ring. Set both to 0 km for the exact spot. After the pin drops, moving it yourself keeps your spot.

Place names come from OpenStreetMap. The extension does not show the raw coordinates.
